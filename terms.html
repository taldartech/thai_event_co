Enterprise Contracting • Commercial Standards

Terms of Service & Master Agreement.

Standard commercial terms, scope governance, force majeure contingencies, and payment milestones governing corporate event production contracts executed by Thai Event Collective.

1. Enterprise Master Services Agreement (MSA)

All productions managed by Thai Event Collective are formalized through an Enterprise Master Services Agreement (MSA) paired with an itemized Scope of Work (SOW). No work commences, venue reservations are finalized, or equipment is blocked without a countersigned agreement.

2. Milestone Payment Schedule & Official Remittance

Unless alternative credit terms are agreed via enterprise vendor procurement, our standard commercial payment schedule is structured as follows:

  • 50% Initial Deposit: Due upon contract execution to secure venue dates, lock technical rigging hardware, and initiate 3D CAD pre-visualizations.
  • 40% Production Milestone: Due 30 days prior to event load-in to cover custom CNC staging fabrication, printing, and subcontractor bookings.
  • 10% Final Settlement: Due within 7 business days following successful event completion and reconciliation of approved incidental orders.

3. Safety, Rigging Certifications & Liability

The Agency guarantees that all aluminum trusses, ground-supported grids, video wall ballasts, and electrical distribution systems are designed and certified by licensed structural engineers in compliance with international event safety standards. Thai Event Collective maintains comprehensive public liability insurance with coverage up to USD 5,000,000 per occurrence.

4. Force Majeure & Weather Contingencies

Neither party shall be held liable for failure or delay in performance caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including acts of God, extreme severe tropical typhoons, governmental bans, or civil emergencies. For coastal outdoor events in Phuket, Thai Event Collective builds mandatory rapid clear-span canopy contingencies and dual generator fail-safes into every production contract to mitigate weather disruption without event cancellation.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

Unless an international arbitration forum (e.g., SIAC Singapore International Arbitration Centre) is specified in your bilateral corporate MSA, contracts are governed by the commercial laws of the Kingdom of Thailand.
