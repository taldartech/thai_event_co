Privacy Policy & Data Governance.

Thai Event Collective is committed to safeguarding corporate client confidentiality, proprietary business assets, and delegate personal data in full compliance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) B.E. 2562 (2019) and global privacy standards.

1. Scope of Data Governance

This policy applies to all Request for Proposals (RFPs), enterprise contracts, delegate registration rosters, live event biometric/RFID badge scanning systems, and digital correspondence managed by Thai Event Collective ("the Agency"). We recognize the sensitive nature of international summits, product unveilings, and executive boardroom discussions.

2. Non-Disclosure & Enterprise Confidentiality (NDA)

All project scopes, technical blueprints, pre-released product specifications, guest rosters, and keynote materials shared with Thai Event Collective are treated as strictly confidential under bilateral non-disclosure protocols. Our production crew, rigging engineers, and technical contractors operate under signed confidentiality agreements.

3. Collection & Use of Delegate Information

When coordinating delegate transport, hotel reservations, or badge credentials, we collect only necessary logistical identifiers (e.g., delegate full name, corporate title, dietary constraints, arrival flight coordinates). This data is stored on encrypted enterprise servers and deleted post-event unless retention is mandated by Thai statutory taxation laws.

4. Media Capture & Broadcast Rights

Unless explicitly authorized in your corporate Master Services Agreement (MSA), Thai Event Collective does not publicly publish event photography, video archives, or client logos. High-profile productions remain private and closed to external media unless direct press clearance is granted by your corporate communications director.
