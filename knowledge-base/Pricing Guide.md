## Pricing philosophy

1. **Custom quotations only** after date, venue, guest count, and scope are clear.
2. **Impact-first budgeting** for décor: prioritise entrances, ceremonies, stages, and dining floors; then secondary accents.
3. **Private / intimate events:** fewer, higher-impact details often outperform thin full-room coverage.
4. **Transparent budget tracking** for corporate and destination planning engagements.
5. Protect budgets from avoidable change orders through clear creative↔technical communication.

## Factors that influence cost

### Décor
- Guest count and table count
- Floral density and stem mix (local tropical vs premium imports)
- Custom backdrops, mandap-style frames, stages
- Indoor ballroom vs beach/garden (weather structures, anchoring)
- Venue access windows, load restrictions, multiple move-ins
- Peak season and public holidays
- Multi-day installs and overnight security/crew
- Lighting complexity (décor lighting vs full show lighting)

### Planning / production
- Event type (wedding vs conference vs activation)
- Single day vs multi-day itinerary
- Vendor volume and VIP protocols
- Hybrid/livestream technical needs
- Permits (public activations)
- Guest transport and hospitality complexity
- Change orders after production confirmation

2. Optionally cite an Approximate band from this guide matching the described tier.
3. List the cost drivers that will move the quote up or down.
4. Ask for date, venue, guest count, and must-have zones.

## Budget prioritisation tips (from site practice)

- **Weddings:** Ceremony entrance, altar/backdrop, stage, and dining floor first.
- **Corporate:** Brand arrival moment, stage/logo, and guest hospitality zones first.
- **Private luxury:** Florals, lighting, gifts, and service choreography over spreading budget thinly.
