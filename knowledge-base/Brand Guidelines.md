# Brand Guidelines

Applies to both **Thai Decor Collective** and **Thai Event Collective** unless a brand-specific note is stated.

---

## Tone of Voice

- Polished luxury hospitality English—formal-warm, never casual slang or emoji-heavy copy.
- Confident and calm; emphasise craft, culture, and operational reliability together.
- Decor brand leans sensory and design-led (“design language,” “continuous narrative,” “authentic Thai artistry”).
- Event brand leans operational and guest-experience-led (“production calendar,” “calm leadership,” “supplier fluency”).
- Prefer British-leaning spellings where the website uses them: *specialises*, *programmes*, *organiser*, *décor*.
- Avoid hype, exaggerated claims, or invented awards. Prefer clear benefits and process clarity.

---

## Brand Values

1. **Cultural authenticity** — Drawing inspiration from Thailand’s cultural heritage; respect first in sacred and temple-inspired settings.
2. **Craftsmanship** — Intricate design, considered materials, cohesive colour and floral language.
3. **Modern luxury** — Contemporary styling with subtle Thai accents; restraint over excess.
4. **Professional discipline** — Beautiful rooms delivered with professional discipline; timelines, access windows, and venue rules respected.
5. **Clear communication** — Listen carefully, communicate clearly, and protect both creative vision and practical constraints.
6. **Operational calm** — Especially for Event Collective: calm leadership, foresight for venue risk, invisible excellence on the day.
7. **Sister-brand integration** — Planning and styling work as one system when both firms are engaged.

## Design Philosophy

- Cohesive design language across ceremony and reception so moments feel like one continuous narrative.
- Place-responsive design: beach vs ballroom vs garden vs temple each get different structural and floral logic—not one style forced onto every venue.
- Beauty plus logistics: structures that photograph well *and* respect wind, humidity, load-in, and resort rules.
- Impact-first budgeting: prioritise entrances, ceremonies, stages, and dining floors before secondary accents.
- For sacred spaces: cultural respect first, visual beauty second; clear pathways for ritual and elders.

---

## Customer Promise

We listen carefully, design with cultural intelligence and modern luxury, communicate clearly through the process, and deliver beautiful rooms and well-run programmes with professional discipline—so couples and brands can host celebrations that feel distinctly Thai and impeccably produced.

When décor and planning are both required, Thai Decor Collective and Thai Event Collective work as sister companies so creative vision and production stay aligned.
