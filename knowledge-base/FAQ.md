## Getting started & booking

### What is Thai wedding decoration?
Thai wedding decoration is the art of styling ceremonies and receptions with Thai cultural elements—florals such as orchids and jasmine, gold accents, traditional motifs, elegant backdrops, and stage design—to create a warm, refined celebration rooted in Thailand's aesthetic heritage.

### What is a Thai event planner?
A Thai event planner manages celebrations in Thailand from concept to on-site delivery—covering venues, vendors, timelines, guest experience and production—so hosts can focus on the occasion while professionals handle the details.

## Timelines & peak season

### When is peak wedding season in Thailand?
Peak demand often aligns with popular travel and cool/dry periods and major holidays; book earlier for Phuket, Samui and Bangkok peak weekends. Exact seasonality varies by destination—ask in consultation.

### How long does installation usually take?
Depends on scope and venue access windows—from several hours for focal installs to full-day or multi-shift builds for full-venue luxury styling.

### How long before guests should install finish?
Aim to complete quiet install, lighting focus and photographer testing before guest arrival; exact call times appear on the production schedule.

### When does dismantling happen?
Usually after guest departure and within venue curfew/access rules—confirmed on the run-of-show.

### How long does concept design take?
Typically follows consultation and venue clarity; revisions occur before production confirmation. Complex multi-day programmes need more lead time.

### Can we change the design after approval?
Changes after production confirmation may affect cost and feasibility; communicate ASAP for a revised quotation.
