## Company Story

Thai Decor Collective is a leading Thai wedding décor company specializing in Thai event decorations, traditional Thai décor, and luxury Thai theme decorations. Drawing inspiration from Thailand’s cultural heritage, the team crafts intricate designs that leave a lasting impression. The commitment is premium event styling and destination wedding décor that reflects authentic Thai artistry.

Thai Event Collective is the sister firm focused on end-to-end planning and production—venue sourcing, vendor coordination, guest experience, run-of-show, and on-site leadership. Integration between planning (Event Collective) and styling (Decor Collective) prevents last-minute conflicts and keeps creative vision aligned with practical constraints.

Together they collaborate with hotels, venues, wedding photographers, planners, and floral suppliers.
