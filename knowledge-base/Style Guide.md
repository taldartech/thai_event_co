## Floral Style

**Signature Thai florals:** orchids, jasmine, lotus accents, tropical foliage.

**Also used:** roses and European garden luxury looks; many celebrations blend Thai botanicals with contemporary garden luxury.

**Installation goals:**
- Look lush at guest arrival and remain beautiful through the final toast.
- Tropical-ready mechanics (heat, humidity, wind).
- Optional post-event flower redistribution where possible.

**Personal flowers:** bridal bouquets and guest/party florals as scoped.

## Decoration Themes

| Theme family | Character |
|--------------|-----------|
| Classic Thai wedding | Orchids, jasmine, floral arches, gold & maroon heritage accents |
| Contemporary luxury Thai | Refined Thai accents in gold or lacquered finishes; restraint |
| Traditional / temple-inspired | Ivory, deep red, gold, botanical greens; carved motifs, fabric draping; reverence first |
| Beach / destination | Light structures, wind-aware installs, ocean or garden backdrops |
| Garden / royal reception | Botanical romance, stage gardens, soft evening light |
| Corporate / brand | Logo integration, branded entrances, media walls, strategic hospitality |
| Intimate luxury | Fewer, higher-impact details; candle-rich tables; quiet excellence |

Colour, texture, floral density, aisle rhythm, and stage presence are planned together so ceremony and reception feel like one narrative.

---

## Luxury Standards

- True luxury is precision: balanced proportions, considered materials, and a room that feels calm even when every detail is elevated.
- Statement floral installations, couture backdrops, candle-rich table landscapes, mirrored surfaces, textured fabrics, elevated centrepieces.
- Consistency from invitation-level experience through farewell (especially luxury / private events with Event Collective).
- White-glove staffing and operations that stay invisible to guests.
- Quiet install; lighting focus; photographer testing before guests arrive.
- Venue returned in excellent condition after breakdown.

---

## Cultural Guidelines

1. **Respect first** in temple and sacred or semi-sacred settings—décor supports reverence, family tradition, and ceremonial order.
2. Soft florals, careful colour choices, and symbolic accents without overwhelming the spiritual heart.
3. Keep pathways clear for ritual movement and elder accessibility.
4. Secure permissions and timing with temple or venue authorities before install.
5. Traditional motifs and temple-inspired detailing communicate hospitality and elegance—adapt scale and materials for modern hotel venues when needed.
6. For international couples adding Thai traditions: authenticity with clear explanation of ceremonial flow when planning support is engaged.
7. Never caricature Thai culture; prefer authentic craftsmanship language over costume-like theming.

---

## Backdrop & Stage Notes

- Backdrops are often the most photographed element—consider height, symmetry, depth, fabric behaviour, floral density, and lighting response.
- Stages balance aesthetics with technical needs: sightlines, microphones, screens, cable routes, presenter movement.
- In Thai celebrations, stages often host blessings, classical dance, speeches, and cake ceremonies.
- Collaborate with AV/producers; final dressing after tech checks when possible.

---

## Destination / Tropical Realities

Design for wind, humidity, heat, and sudden rain rather than forcing indoor ballroom styles outdoors. Use wind-aware structures, backup placements, flexible lighting, and contingency plans for beach and lawn venues.
