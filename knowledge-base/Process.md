# Process

Unified workflow for Thai Decor Collective (décor) and Thai Event Collective (planning/production). Stages may compress for intimate events or expand for multi-day destination programmes.

---

## Workflow Overview

```
Inquiry
  ↓
Consultation
  ↓
Venue Visit
  ↓
Concept Design
  ↓
Quotation
  ↓
Approval
  ↓
Production
  ↓
Setup
  ↓
Event Day
  ↓
Dismantling
```

---

## 1. Inquiry

**What happens:** Client reaches out with date, location preferences, approximate guest count, and objectives (or décor priorities).

**How to inquire:**
- Email: team@thaidecor.co
- Phone / WhatsApp: +66 81-149-0924
- Calendly: https://calendly.com/team-thaidecor/30min

**Helpful to include:** venue or shortlist, guest count, colour preferences, ceremony/cultural requirements, success metrics (for corporate), multi-day timeline if destination.

---

## 2. Consultation

**What happens:** Discovery call or meeting. The team listens carefully—venue realities, culture, taste, budget clarity, and constraints.

**Decor focus:** Guest entry path, photographer positions, catering/ceremonial access routes, impact zones (entrances, ceremony, stage, dining).

**Event focus:** Shared stakeholder plan (couples, marketing, execs, venues, suppliers); document decisions; track dependencies; concise communication.

**Temple / cultural note:** Permissions, ritual order, and elder accessibility discussed early.

---

## 3. Venue Visit

**What happens:** Site survey (in person or supported remotely for international clients). Measurements, power, rigging, load-in routes, acoustics, ceiling heights, access rules, noise curfews, weather exposure.

**Decor:** Scale drawings for backdrops/stages; tropical and wind considerations for outdoor sites.

**Event:** Venue shortlist by style and guest count; contract guidance; technical riders; capacity and hospitality flow.

---

## 4. Concept Design

**What happens:** Creative direction is presented for approval.

**Decor deliverables:** Moodboards, floor plans, colour stories, floral concepts, installation sketches; cohesive design language across ceremony and reception.

**Event deliverables:** Programme design, guest experience map (arrival → welcome → peaks → hospitality pauses → departure), vendor shortlist aligned to scale/brand/budget.

**Luxury décor path:** Private consultation → concept → refine budgets, materials, and installation logistics.

**Destination wedding method:** Vision + budget → venue shortlist → design / catering / entertainment / ceremony → production calendar + guest logistics.

---

## 5. Quotation

**What happens:** Tailored proposal based on scope—floral density, structures, install complexity, destination logistics, production needs.

**Notes:** No fixed public packages. Impact-first budgeting recommended. Banking details for deposits: K-bank `198-3-89376-3`, Swift `KASITHBK`.

**GPT guidance:** Point to Pricing Guide for Approximate ranges; never invent a firm total—invite consultation.

---

## 6. Approval

**What happens:** Client approves concept and quotation (with revisions as needed). Production confirmation milestone locked.

**Temple page milestones (example):** discovery call → concept presentation → revisions → production confirmation → installation day briefing.

**Critical path owners assigned:** venue confirmation, technical riders, guest transport, rehearsal windows, permits (activations), weather contingency.

---

## 7. Production

**What happens:** Materials sourced; off-site construction as needed; supplier briefing before load-in; stem counts and tropical mechanics for florals; stage plots, equipment lists, run-of-show, cue sheets.

**Event:** Production calendar; vendor QC through rehearsals; protect budgets from avoidable change orders via clear creative↔technical communication.

**Decor:** Build around venue access windows; structural reuse where sustainable.

---

## 8. Setup (Installation)

**What happens:** On-site build within venue access windows. Quiet install; lighting focus; early backdrop install for photographer/lighting testing when possible.

**Stage:** Load-in → rehearsal → final dressing after tech checks.

**Outdoor:** Anchoring, weather backups, generator/redundancy as scoped.

**Roles may include:** venue liaison, décor lead, stage/AV partners.

---

## 9. Event Day

**What happens:** On-site leadership; run-of-show; adjustments as needed; guest experience protected.

**Event command roles:** venue liaison, stage cues, VIP arrivals.

**Decor:** Stay for adjustments; beautiful rooms delivered with professional discipline.

**Luxury standard:** Operations remain calm and largely invisible to guests.

---

## 10. Dismantling (Wrap)

**What happens:** Breakdown; venue returned in excellent condition; optional flower redistribution; vendor reconciliation; practical debrief / lessons learned; wrap reporting (conferences/corporate).

---

## Sister-Brand Handoff

When both firms are engaged:
1. Event Collective owns planning timeline, vendors, guest logistics, and production command.
2. Decor Collective owns styling concepts, florals, stages, backdrops, and install/breakdown of décor.
3. Integration prevents last-minute conflicts between creative vision and practical constraints.
