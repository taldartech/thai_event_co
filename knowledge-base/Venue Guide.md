## Universal venue survey checklist

- Ceiling height and hanging points  
- Load-in route and lift size  
- Power and rigging  
- Indoor vs weather exposure  
- Noise curfew  
- Candle / open-flame policy  
- Florist and external vendor rules  
- Guest capacity vs décor footprint  
- Photographer positions and backdrop height  
- Ritual or brand access routes
