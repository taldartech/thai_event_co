## Service: Traditional Thai Decor

**URL:** https://thaidecor.co/decor/traditional-thai-decor.html

**Description:**  
Traditional Thai décor carries centuries of artistic language: graceful curves, symbolic colour, floral reverence, and architectural ornament. Authenticity is respected while scale and materials are adapted for modern venues. Orchids, jasmine garlands, gold accents, carved motifs, and fabric draping are common. Colour stories often include ivory, deep red, gold, and botanical greens.

**Best For:**
- Thai wedding ceremonies and blessings
- Temple-inspired sacred moments

- Multi-generational families
- International couples adding Thai traditions

**Deliverables:**

- Focal motifs at ceremony backdrops, stage frames, entrance portals, table accents

## Service: Temple Decorations

**URL:** https://thaidecor.co/decor/temple-decorations.html

**Description:**  
Temple-inspired celebrations require sensitivity. Décor should support reverence, family tradition, and ceremonial order—cultural respect first and visual beauty second. Soft florals, careful colour choices, and symbolic accents without overwhelming the spiritual heart. Pathways stay clear for ritual movement and elder accessibility.

**Best For:**
- Temple ceremonies
- Hotel rooms inspired by temple architecture
- Blessing corners within larger weddings
- Destination cultural segments
- Heritage-inspired receptions

**Deliverables:**
- Blessing florals
- Entrance garlands
- Altar-adjacent florals with ritual access
- Hotel ceremony cultural décor
- Family photo temple-inspired frames
- Soft evening blessing lighting
- Coordinated post-ceremony reception accents
- Planning milestones: discovery → concept → revisions → production confirmation → installation briefing
- Materials/sustainability: reuse of structural frames where appropriate
