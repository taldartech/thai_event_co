About Our Sister Brands

Thai Decor Collective specialises in Thai wedding decorations, traditional Thai décor, floral installations, stage and backdrop design, and luxury destination wedding styling across Thailand.

Thai Event Collective is a full-service Thai event planner delivering destination weddings, corporate events, product launches, conference management, exhibition support, brand activations and event production.

Short Boilerplate (40–50 words)

Thai Decor Collective and Thai Event Collective are sister companies creating luxury celebrations across Thailand. From Thai wedding decorations and floral design to destination wedding planning and corporate event production, the teams help couples and brands deliver culturally rich, professionally executed experiences in Bangkok, Phuket and beyond.

Long Boilerplate ( foreditorial features)

Based in Thailand, Thai Decor Collective designs authentic and luxury Thai event decorations for weddings, ceremonies and corporate occasions. Its sister firm, Thai Event Collective, provides end-to-end event management for destination weddings, MICE programmes, gala dinners and brand experiences. Together they collaborate with hotels, venues, wedding photographers, planners and floral suppliers to deliver celebrations that feel distinctly Thai and impeccably produced.
