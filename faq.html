Thai Event Collective FAQ

Clear answers about event planning, destination weddings, corporate events and production in Thailand.

What is a Thai event planner?

A Thai event planner manages celebrations in Thailand from concept to on-site delivery—covering venues, vendors, timelines, guest experience and production—so hosts can focus on the occasion while professionals handle the details.

How much does event planning cost?

Fees depend on event type, guest count, venue complexity and production needs. Destination weddings, conferences and product launches each have different scopes. Share your brief and we will provide a clear planning proposal.

How early should I book?

For destination weddings, book 6–12 months ahead when possible. Corporate events and conferences are ideally planned 3–9 months in advance. Peak seasons in Thailand fill quickly, so earlier is better.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. We support international couples, brands and planners hosting events in Thailand. We act as your local event organiser for destination weddings, corporate programmes and luxury celebrations nationwide.

What services do you provide?

We provide corporate events, destination weddings, product launches, conference management, exhibition management, brand activation, private events, luxury events and full event production support.

Can you plan both weddings and corporate events?

Yes. Thai Event Collective specialises in both. Whether you need a destination wedding or a corporate gala, conference or retreat, we tailor planning and production to your goals.

Do you handle venue sourcing in Thailand?

Yes. We help shortlist and secure hotels, resorts, rooftops, gardens and unique venues that fit your guest count, budget and event style across major Thai destinations.

Can you coordinate décor and styling too?

Yes. Through our sister company, Thai Decor Collective, we can align planning with Thai wedding decorations, floral design, stages and backdrops for a fully integrated look.

Do you manage on-site event production?

Yes. Our team provides on-site management, run-of-show leadership and event production coordination—including staging and AV partners—so your programme runs on time.

How do we get started?

Send your event date, location preferences, approximate guest count and objectives. We will schedule a consultation and outline the best planning path for your celebration or corporate programme.
