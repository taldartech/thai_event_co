Corporate FAQ

Essential operational information for international conference directors and corporate planners.

How far in advance should we submit an RFP?

For large-scale international conferences (500+ delegates) and high-season summits (November to April), we recommend engaging our agency 3 to 12 months in advance to secure optimal venue space, technical rigging permits, and line-array sound systems.

Do you operate across both Bangkok and Phuket?

Yes. Thai Event Collective operates as your premier Corporate Event Organizer in Bangkok and leading Corporate Event Planner in Phuket. We maintain permanent production fleets, equipment warehouses, and technical teams in both hubs, executing events across Koh Samui, Chiang Mai, and Krabi.

Why choose us as your Event Management Company in Thailand?

As an accredited Event Management Company in Thailand and premier Destination Management Company in Thailand, we offer client-first peace of mind: a single dedicated executive producer, 100% transparent line-item pricing with zero hidden surcharges, and dual-redundancy AV systems.

Can you manage full Conference Management in Thailand?

Absolutely. Our Conference Management in Thailand division oversees plenary stage construction, digital check-in systems, simultaneous translation booths, speaker green-room concierges, and broadcast-grade technical staging for international summits.
